OEE Calculation Framework

Overall Equipment Effectiveness identifies the proportion of manufacturing time that is truly productive. The system keeps raw Performance visible as a data-quality signal while using capped Performance for OEE so abnormal cycle-time entries do not inflate the headline KPI.
